Pho Recipe

Pho is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup that is loved around the world. This recipe will guide you through making the perfect bowl of pho with tender beef, aromatic spices, and fresh herbs. I like to eat this when it is cold outside or I have a stuffy nose since the hot broth will help you deal with those symptoms.

  • Ingredients:
    • 2 large onions
    • 150g/5oz ginger
    • 10 star anise
    • 4 cinnamon quills
