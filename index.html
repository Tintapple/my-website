Aliens from the school parking lot

Eye witness accounts

I was walking across the school parking lot after the final bell when I heard a strange humming sound above me. I looked up and saw a huge silver object hovering over the parking lot. It slowly descended and landed without making a sound, and a bright white light came from underneath it. Then, two strange creatures stepped out. They were very thin, with long arms, gray suits, and huge dark eyes. One of them looked directly at me and raised its hand. I froze in fear until a teacher ran outside and told everyone to get back inside. Suddenly, the blue lights around the spaceship became brighter, and the aliens climbed back inside. The spaceship lifted into the air and disappeared into the clouds within seconds. When everyone came outside later, there was a strange circular mark left on the pavement. I know what I saw, and I will never forget it.
